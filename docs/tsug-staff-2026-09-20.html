TL;DR

Fixed moveProduct() to surface RPC errors instead of silently falling through to a broken client-side upsert fallback. Added a "Reorder" toggle with @dnd-kit drag-and-drop that calls the reorder_products RPC for bulk category reordering with optimistic UI. Removed dead sort_order fallbacks (migration 0020 is live). Removed the "Show details" accordion and Refresh button from the orders list, surfacing rider Assign/Reassign and Kitchen Status directly on each order card. Made the inventory actions column sticky so Edit/Stock controls are always visible at narrower viewports.
